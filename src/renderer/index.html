Files and privacy

Attach PDF, Word, RTF, text, CSV, TSV, JSON, or XLSX files. Up to 8 files, 25 MB each, and 200,000 extracted characters per chat. PDF scans use on-device OCR on macOS. Windows requires PDFs with selectable text. Images and charts are not interpreted.

Your questions, extracted files, and the conversation are shared with both providers through your existing CLI logins. The app saves chat history on this computer. If an assistant doesn’t recognize something your question depends on, it searches the web before the discussion; its search sees your question and clarification answers, but not your files. File editing and other tools are off.
